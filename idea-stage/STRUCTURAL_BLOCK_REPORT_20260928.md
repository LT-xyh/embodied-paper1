# Structural search block after TCI

**Status:** `BLOCKED — STRUCTURAL RE-IDEATION REQUIRED`

This report records why the current autonomous search cannot responsibly advance a Paper-1 candidate without changing the research scope or receiving a new value/resource decision. It does not claim that embodied AI lacks open problems; it records that the current constrained route has exhausted the candidate families that can be falsified on the qualified state-only robosuite/robomimic substrate in one to three days.

## Evidence chain

1. **Substrate-first waves:** the project’s two substrate-first searches found no candidate that simultaneously had an independent replication path, a non-audit scientific claim, and a low-risk public execution substrate. Armnet and SocNav evidence was preserved as benchmark-specific negative evidence.
2. **Baseline-first search:** the mature robomimic/robosuite/MuJoCo state-only stack was admitted, but the 2025–2026 robot-learning prior audit found no direct method gap that was both nontrivial and executable within the overnight budget.
3. **Adjacent-field transfer:** recent mechanisms from sequence modeling, offline RL, representation learning, distribution shift, adaptation, and optimization were searched against robotics terminology. The surviving compositions either directly collided with robotics work or reduced to a loss/benchmark change.
4. **Track-A reset candidate:** transition-surprise hard invalidation of a recurrent BC-RNN was admitted only for a bounded P0. The runtime was repaired and a real frozen BC-RNN gate was completed. Its held-out consequence inversion was weak/chance, multi-horizon had no advantage, and shuffled controls were comparable. An independent review finalized `FAIL — TCI CONSEQUENCE IDENTIFIABILITY`.
5. **Current structural re-ideation:** bounded-support BC, delay-calibrated BC, contact-transition gradient routing, phase/speed conditioning, and control-informed action metrics were audited. An independent `gpt-6-luna / max` review returned `ZERO / ABANDON`:
   * bounded-support BC is the known scale-adjusted truncated-Gaussian/action-head change, with a close robot clipping prior and no identified mismatch on the frozen deterministic tanh BC-RNN;
   * delay calibration is directly covered by Delay-Aware Diffusion Policy, RAPAC-DP, delayed BC/action-chunking analysis, RACE, and SAIL;
   * transition-conditioned gradient routing is directly covered by GAP and surrounding contact-conditioned optimization work;
   * phase and physical-loss variants are already covered or are generic loss/metric swaps.

## Why no pilot is authorized

The only remaining bounded-action diagnostic used a deliberately saturating legal scripted trace and found boundary mass in that trace. It did not establish pre-controller clipping in the frozen BC-RNN, and it did not compare policies. Starting a training pilot would therefore substitute a new probabilistic policy family to create the premise that the candidate requires. That would violate the measurement-first and no-rescue rules.

No method implementation, policy training, new dataset/checkpoint download, runtime installation, GPU/DCU job, video processing, or scientific P0 was launched in this re-ideation cycle. The existing qualified CPU simulator was used only for the boundary diagnostic.

## Scope exhausted

The following scientific objects are now negative or disallowed for this project unless a genuinely new mechanism is specified:

* hidden-state memory, transition surprise, action-swap consequence consistency, or deterministic policy preference;
* generic latency, action-chunk timing, phase conditioning, recovery/retry, and horizon adaptation;
* generic mixed-quality/demo selection, safety/failure monitoring, world-model evaluation, contact-topology transfer, or benchmark audits;
* generic gradient surgery, quantization, bounded-distribution/loss swaps, Jacobian-weighted MSE, or optimizer changes.

Continuing to rename or combine these families would not be a structural search and would produce a predictable direct-prior or identifiability failure. A new attempt requires one of: a new scientific object outside this list, a different publication/compute scope, or an explicitly chosen empirical/evaluation paper target. That decision is outside the current autonomous route.
