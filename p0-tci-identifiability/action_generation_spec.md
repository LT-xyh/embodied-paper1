# TCI action-generation specification

- Policy-facing input: canonical state-only robosuite Lift/Panda/BASIC observation at a captured wrapper state.
- Measurement policy: frozen deterministic scripted controller used only as a measurement stub, not a BC-RNN or TCI method: `a_hat[:3] = clip(4*(cube_pos-eef_pos), -0.35, 0.35)`, zero rotation, neutral gripper.
- Alternatives: four executable actions generated by fixed-angle orthogonal rotations of the translational action vector (`theta=0.25` radians), preserving absolute norm and distance to the chosen action. No clipping was applied after rotation; all actions were checked against the controller bounds.
- Candidate order: independently permuted per state with seed `20260928 + state_ordinal*7919`; chosen-position counts were 6, 8, 8, 8, 10 across five positions.
- The chosen action is selected before any consequence is observed. No policy likelihood, preference, confidence, or post-hoc outcome is used.
