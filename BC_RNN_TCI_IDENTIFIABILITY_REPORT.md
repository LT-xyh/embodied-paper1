# Policy-faithful BC-RNN TCI identifiability gate

## Final verdict

**`FAIL — TCI CONSEQUENCE IDENTIFIABILITY`**

The identified preflight defect was repaired with a real deterministic recurrent
imitation policy. The repair establishes that the consequence measurement is
reproducible and that a non-degenerate BC-RNN can be queried at a fixed hidden
state. It does not establish TCI's frozen temporal-inversion hypothesis: on
held-out evaluation states, multi-horizon ranking is exactly tied with the
immediate ranking and the preregistered inversion event remains absent.

## Policy artifact and reproducibility

No public robomimic checkpoint was present in the qualified environment, so the
smallest legitimate artifact permitted by the objective was trained once with
ordinary CPU PyTorch. The policy is a single-layer 64-hidden-unit GRU followed
by a tanh seven-dimensional action head. Demonstrations were generated only to
provide behavior-cloning data; the factual policy used for every measurement is
the trained recurrent network. Training used 12 episodes from seeds 200--205,
validation used four disjoint episodes from seeds 206--207, and the fixed
training seed was 20260928. The frozen checkpoint, normalization statistics,
configuration, and metrics are in `p0-tci-bcrnn-identifiability/`.

The fixed training produced action RMSE `0.00230` on its demonstration episodes
and `0.00511` on validation episodes. Validation predicted-action standard
deviation was `0.1173`, so the policy is non-degenerate. No tuning was performed
against TCI outcomes.

For each decision point, the policy input is the genuine canonical observation
history from reset through that point. The actual GRU hidden state and action
are reconstructed twice before freezing the decision state. Across all 40
states, the maximum repeated hidden-state error, repeated-action error, and
stored-action error are all `0.0`.

## Intervention and state independence

The evaluation has 40 unique states, with 20 evaluation states in each split.
Evaluation seeds are 100--103 and are disjoint from the demonstration seeds.
Whole episodes remain in one split: seeds 100/102 are evaluation-train and
101/103 are held-out test. Native-state hash overlap is zero. Four valid
equal-radius alternatives are generated by the unchanged fixed-angle
orthogonal-rotation construction. The maximum per-state norm mismatch is
`3.89e-15`, and the maximum within-state chosen-distance mismatch is
`4.72e-16`; every candidate is inside normalized action bounds.

Every candidate is evaluated by restoring the same complete wrapper-level state
in a fresh subprocess. After the candidate action, H=4 and H=12 use the same
frozen BC-RNN queried on each branch's subsequent observation, so these are
policy continuations rather than the prior scripted-controller continuation.
The task consequence is unchanged: `G_H` combines EEF-to-cube distance, cube
height change, and cumulative reward. TCI inversion remains a margin-qualified
sign change between H=1 and H=12 with margin `0.005`.

## Results

The five-way chance top-1 rate is `0.20`. On the held-out 20-state split:

| criterion | top-1 |
|---|---:|
| immediate H=1 consequence | 0.35 |
| rank-aggregated H={1,4,12} consequence | 0.35 |
| within-state shuffled consequence | 0.15 |

The paired multi-horizon minus immediate difference is `0.00` with bootstrap
interval `[-0.20, 0.20]`. The genuine result is above chance and separates from
the shuffled control, which establishes a real immediate consequence signal.
However, the multi-horizon signal provides no advantage over H=1. The required
short/long inversion occurs in `0/40` states, including `0/20` held-out states.
Evaluation-train states have immediate top-1 `0.20` and multi-horizon top-1
`0.40`; this does not rescue the held-out temporal claim.

The fresh-process null control remains exact: maximum consequence error `0.0`
and maximum restored-observation error `0.0` at tolerance `1e-10`. Candidate
position counts are `6, 8, 8, 8, 10`; no identifiers, position, action norm,
or duplicate state can explain the effect.

## Scientific decision

The real BC-RNN rerun removes the only prior scope objection. It also makes the
negative temporal result scientifically interpretable. A non-degenerate,
history-conditioned recurrent policy produces a measurable held-out immediate
consequence relation, but no reproducible evidence that a longer temporal
consequence changes the ranking or creates the registered inversion. This is a
failure of the frozen TCI mechanism, not a reason to increase capacity, expand
horizons, add an auxiliary objective, redefine the consequence, or tune the
policy. If the independent reviewer confirms these controls, the correct action
is `ABANDON TCI` and structural re-ideation under the accumulated negative
evidence policy.
