Independent review: gpt-6-astra / medium; thread /root/new_direction_reviewer. CAEA = PROCEED WITH CAUTION, eligible only for a tightly specified runtime-qualified pilot. It is distinct from CMA because it holds history/present fixed and tests policy action preference against real simulator successor value; distinct from WorldEcho/WorldSync because it uses frozen policy plus ground-truth transitions, not learned world models; distinct from PACT because it is an audit without human corrective labels or policy updates; distinct from PAM/MEMBOT/JEPA/transition-surprise memory because it has no memory mechanism or auxiliary loss. Strongest objection: continuous BC-RNN often lacks a calibrated score over candidate actions, and nearest-action or action-distance effects can make the result a metric rephrasing. Require equal-radius balanced candidates, score-identifiability gate, preregistered successor value, fresh-process cloning, common seeds, and controls for random, nearest-demo, Euclidean distance, next-state predictor, transition surprise, contrastive/action-conditioned representation, adaptive memory, and CMA history swaps. Positive requires cross-task/seed rank alignment and failure lead-time beyond controls; null means policy scores are not locally causal; negative means one-task, horizon/tolerance fragile, or explained by distance/contact and kills the claim. AEPAA = ABANDON: high collision with action redundancy/functional-equivalence literature; researcher-defined horizon/tolerance classes and within-class instability collapse to action variance/robustness metrics. CDBST = ABANDON as generic perturbation robustness. Recommend only CAEA to a later pilot, with score-identifiability as a hard pre-implementation gate. No implementation or experiments were run.
